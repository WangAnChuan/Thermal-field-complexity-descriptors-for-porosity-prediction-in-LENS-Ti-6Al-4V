Figure 1. Train/test partitions of the 60 layers under the three evaluation protocols (panel (a) shows the real seed-0 split of all 1564 frames: 1094 training, 470 test; the build has 16–34 frames per layer). (a) Stratified 70/30 random split (other seeds give different realisations): frames from every layer fall in both sets, so temporally adjacent frames leak across the split (adjacency open) and position can be used as a proxy (position open); SMOTE is applied to the training portion only. (b) Layer-grouped 5-fold: whole layers are held out, which closes adjacency leakage, but training layers lie on both sides of every test layer, so positional proxies remain usable. (c) Forward chaining: models are trained on layers 1–k and tested on layers k+1 to k+10, so both channels are closed. The stability analysis uses k = 15, 20, …, 45; the k = 50 test window ends at the last layer.
